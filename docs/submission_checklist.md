## Source-of-truth note

The official project requirements available in this workspace require at least 30 troubleshooting cases covering the listed networking concepts and required case fields. No official Cisco, EdCreate, course, internship, or submission-email source available here explicitly requires 30 verified lab cases. Therefore the first item above is an internal quality goal, not an established official submission requirement. The current `4 VERIFIED + 21 REFERENCE + 5 PENDING` evidence model is acceptable under the official requirements available in this workspace, provided provenance remains visible and no reference evidence is presented as verified.
